## WHAT TO AVOID

❌ **Don't:**
- Make promises about approvals or commissions
- Oversell or exaggerate your platform's capabilities
- Ask for a meeting before establishing value
- Pressure for an immediate decision
- Ignore their objections or concerns

✅ **Do:**
- Focus on mutual benefit
- Be clear about what you're offering
- Respect their time
- Follow up professionally
- Listen to their needs and concerns

- **Meeting Rate** - Percentage who agree to a call

- **Lead Generation** - Actual referrals and leads generated

## PERSONALIZATION TIPS

Before reaching out:
1. **Research the organization** - Understand their products, values, and audience
2. **Find the right contact** - Look for decision-makers (VP of Partnerships, Managing Director, etc.)
3. **Customize the message** - Reference specific products or services they offer
4. **Find common ground** - Mention any shared values or mutual connections
5. **Add local context** - If reaching out to a funeral home, mention your commitment to their community

Example personalization:
"I noticed that [Carrier/Funeral Home] has been focused on serving [community/market], and that's exactly who we're building Self-Made Legends for..."
