Frequently Asked Questions

What is Final Expense insurance? ▼

Final Expense insurance is a type of life insurance designed to help your family cover the costs associated with your death. This includes funeral expenses, burial or cremation costs, and other outstanding debts or expenses. It's a simple, affordable way to ensure your family isn't burdened with these costs during an already difficult time. The benefit is paid directly to your beneficiary, who can use it however they need.

How fast is approval? ▼

We pride ourselves on quick and simple approval. Our streamlined application process means you can get coverage fast. In many cases, you can receive approval and start your coverage within days of submitting your application. We understand that peace of mind is important, so we've designed our process to be as quick and painless as possible while ensuring we have all the information we need.

Do I need a medical exam? ▼

No, you do not need a medical exam to apply for Final Expense insurance. We understand that medical exams can be inconvenient and time-consuming. Our application process is designed to be simple and accessible. You simply answer a few straightforward health questions on our form, and that's all we need. No blood tests, no doctor visits, no hassle. Just honest answers and you're on your way to coverage.

How does this help my family? ▼

Final Expense insurance provides your family with financial protection when they need it most. Instead of scrambling to cover funeral costs, burial expenses, or other debts after you pass away, your family will have the funds to handle these costs with dignity and without financial stress. This means they can focus on grieving and honoring your memory, rather than worrying about money. Your coverage amount goes directly to your beneficiary, giving them the resources to make the decisions that are right for your family.
