Who We Are

Self-Made Legends Life & Legacy Insurance Co. is a modern, Black-owned digital insurance brand dedicated to helping families protect what matters most. We're not a traditional insurance company—we're a digital distribution platform designed for the families that traditional insurance often overlooks or confuses.

Our mission is simple: make life insurance simple, accessible, and affordable for everyone. We believe every family deserves the chance to protect their loved ones without confusion, frustration, or unnecessary bureaucracy.
