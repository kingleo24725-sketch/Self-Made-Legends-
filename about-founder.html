About the Founder

The story behind Self-Made Legends Life & Legacy Insurance Co.

👨‍💼

Jason Brown, Founder

Jason Brown founded Self-Made Legends Life & Legacy Insurance Co. with a simple mission: to make life insurance simple, accessible, and affordable for Black families and communities that have been overlooked by traditional insurance companies.

What started as a personal frustration has become a movement to transform how families protect themselves and build generational wealth.

A company built on three pillars:

Clarity • Compassion • Community

The Story

Jason Brown's journey to founding Self-Made Legends began with a personal moment that changed everything. When a close family member became seriously ill, Jason watched as his family struggled with the complexity and confusion of traditional life insurance.

The experience revealed a painful truth: life insurance—something so essential to protecting family—was designed to confuse, not clarify. Terms were unclear. Processes were complicated. And for Black families and communities, the experience was often dismissive and exclusionary.

Jason knew there had to be a better way. A way that was simple. A way that was respectful. A way that actually served the families and communities that needed it most.

"Insurance companies spend millions convincing people to buy their products. What if we spent that energy making insurance actually simple to understand?"

That insight became the foundation of Self-Made Legends. Jason realized that the problem wasn't life insurance itself—it was how it was being sold and explained. Traditional insurance companies had designed their systems for complexity, not clarity. For profit extraction, not family protection.

Self-Made Legends was built to be different. To put families first. To make protection simple. To honor the dignity of families building their legacy.

Why This Matters

Black families in America have historically been denied access to wealth-building tools like life insurance. Systemic barriers have kept insurance expensive, complicated, and inaccessible. The financial security that life insurance provides—the chance to pass wealth to your family, to ensure your kids' education, to leave a legacy—has been systematically withheld.

Self-Made Legends exists to change that narrative. We believe:

  • Every family deserves protection. Not just wealthy families. Not just connected families. Every family, especially Black families, deserves access to affordable, trusted life insurance.
  • Insurance should be simple. Not confusing. Not designed to trip you up. Simple, transparent, and clear.
  • Businesses can do good and do well. We don't have to choose between being profitable and being purposeful. We can build a sustainable business while serving our community with real integrity.
  • Community is everything. We're not a distant, faceless corporation. We're part of this community. We understand your values. We share your mission.

The Vision

Self-Made Legends is just the beginning. The long-term vision is to become the trusted insurance brand for Black families—not just in Final Expense insurance, but across the full spectrum of life insurance and wealth-building products.

Jason envisions a future where:

  • Black families have access to the same wealth-building tools as everyone else
  • Insurance is a bridge to financial security, not an obstacle
  • Technology and human care work together to serve families better
  • Black-owned businesses are leading the financial services industry
  • Generational wealth becomes normal in Black communities

Every day, the team at Self-Made Legends is working toward that vision. From the engineers who build our technology, to the customer support specialists who answer your questions, to the partnerships we form with carriers and funeral homes—everyone is focused on the same goal: helping families protect what matters most.
