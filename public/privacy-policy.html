🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective: August 17, 2026  ·  Last Updated: August 17, 2026  ·  Self-Made Legends LLC

The short version: We collect only what we need to run the platform, we never sell your data, and you can delete your account anytime. Full details below.

1. Who We Are

Self-Made Legends LLC ("we", "us", "our") operates the Self-Made Legends trading game platform. Questions? Contact us at leobrown24725@yahoo.com.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Full name and email address (when you register)
  • Date of birth (required to verify you are 18 or older)
  • Gender (optional — used to personalize your in-game avatar and profile)
  • Avatar name and tagline (optional — displayed on your public profile)
  • Password (stored as a one-way hash — we never see your actual password)
  • Payment information (processed by Stripe — we never store your card number)
  • Custom crypto token names and descriptions you create
  • AI coach interaction queries you submit (stored to improve coach responses)

Information collected automatically:

  • IP address and general location (for security and fraud prevention)
  • Session activity logs (trades executed, leaderboard scores, badges earned, missions completed)
  • Browser type and device type
  • Casino Chip balances and game outcomes (stored in-game; chips have no monetary value)

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To verify you meet the minimum age requirement of 18 years
  • To run the AI trading engine and display your portfolio
  • To rank you on the weekly leaderboard
  • To process purchases via Stripe
  • To send you email notifications (leaderboard updates, badge alerts, weekly recaps) — you can opt out anytime
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or security threats
  • To improve the platform

4. Payment Processing

All payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store your credit card number, CVV, or full card details on our servers. Stripe's privacy policy applies to payment data: stripe.com/privacy.

5. Information Sharing & Third-Party Processors

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party. We share data only in these limited circumstances:

  • Stripe — payment processing. Your card data is sent directly to Stripe and never stored on our servers. See stripe.com/privacy.
  • Railway.app — cloud hosting and infrastructure. Your data resides on Railway's servers in the US. See railway.app/legal/privacy.
  • AI Coach Provider — coach queries you submit may be processed by an AI service to generate responses. Queries are not stored beyond the current session for model training. The AI provider processes data under a data processing agreement.
  • Email delivery provider — transactional and marketing emails are delivered via a third-party email service (e.g. SendGrid or similar). Your email address is shared with this provider solely to deliver emails you have requested.
  • Legal requirements — if required by law or court order, or to protect our rights and the safety of users.

6. Public Leaderboard

Your username and leaderboard rank are visible to all users on the platform. Your email address, date of birth, and gender are never displayed publicly. You may use a username that does not reveal your real name.

7. Data Security

We protect your data using:

  • Encrypted connections (TLS) for all data in transit
  • Hashed password storage (we cannot recover your password)
  • Rate limiting and intrusion detection
  • Automatic account lockout after failed login attempts

8. Data Retention

We retain data according to the following schedules:

  • Account and profile data — retained while your account is active; deleted within 30 days of account deletion request
  • Payment records — retained for 7 years to comply with financial recordkeeping requirements
  • Server and security logs — retained for 90 days
  • AI coach queries — not retained beyond the current session; not used for model training
  • Audit logs — retained for 2 years for security and compliance purposes

Where retention is required by applicable law (tax, financial, or legal records), we retain that data for the legally required period regardless of account deletion.

9. Your Rights

  • Access — request a copy of your data (or use GET /api/account/export in-app)
  • Correction — update inaccurate information in Account Settings
  • Deletion — delete your account and all personal data (use DELETE /api/account in-app, or email us)
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format (JSON export via the API)
  • Restriction — request that we limit processing of your data while a complaint is pending
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Opt-out of marketing — unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time via the unsubscribe link

To exercise any of these rights, email leobrown24725@yahoo.com. We will respond within 30 days (45 days for complex requests).

10. CCPA — California Residents

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to Know — request the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you
  • Right to Delete — request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal exceptions)
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale — we do not sell your personal information. If this changes, we will provide a "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" link before any such sharing begins.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination — we will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights

To exercise your CCPA rights, contact us at leobrown24725@yahoo.com or write to us at 18615 E Arrowhead Pl, Independence, MO 64056. We will respond within 45 days.

11. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Self-Made Legends is not directed at persons under 18, and specifically not at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 years old. We require date-of-birth verification at registration. If we discover that a user is under 13, we will immediately delete their account and all associated data in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If you believe a minor under 13 has created an account, contact us immediately at leobrown24725@yahoo.com.

12. Cookies

We use session storage to keep you logged in during your browser session. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Note: Stripe, our payment processor, may set cookies on Stripe-hosted checkout pages when you make a purchase. Those cookies are governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy.

12a. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a security breach that compromises your personal information, we will notify you and applicable regulatory authorities as required by law — within 72 hours of discovery for users in the EU/EEA (GDPR Art. 33–34), and within the timeframe required by your state's breach notification law (e.g., 30–45 days for California, New York, and other states with specific requirements). Notification will be sent to the email address associated with your account.

13. GDPR — European Union / EEA Residents

If you are located in the European Union, European Economic Area, or United Kingdom, the following applies to you under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

Lawful Bases for Processing:

  • Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b)) — account creation, portfolio management, trade execution, payment processing
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — security monitoring, fraud prevention, platform improvement
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — marketing emails (you may withdraw consent anytime)
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) — retaining financial records as required by law

International Data Transfers: Your data is stored and processed in the United States. We transfer data from the EU/EEA to the US under Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission with each of our service providers (Stripe, Railway.app).

GDPR Rights: In addition to the rights in §9, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection supervisory authority if you believe we have not handled your data appropriately.
