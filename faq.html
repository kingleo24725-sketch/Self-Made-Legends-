Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about Final Expense insurance? We've got answers. Here's what families usually want to know.

We're here to make life insurance simple and clear. If you don't see your question answered below, please reach out to us anytime.

What is Final Expense insurance?

+

Final Expense insurance is a simple, affordable life insurance product designed to help your family cover funeral costs and other end-of-life expenses. It typically provides a death benefit between $5,000 and $25,000 that your loved ones can use to pay for funeral services, burial, medical bills, and other expenses.

Unlike traditional life insurance, Final Expense insurance is designed to be easier to qualify for, faster to approve, and more affordable. There are no medical exams required, just honest answers to a few simple health questions.

How fast is approval?

+

Most applications are reviewed and approved within 1-2 weeks. Here's our typical timeline:

  • Day 1-2: You complete your quote and application
  • Day 2-7: We review your information
  • Day 7-14: You receive approval and your policy is active

This is much faster than traditional insurance companies, which often take 4-6 weeks or longer. Your approval speed depends on how quickly you submit your application and how straightforward your health information is.

Do I need a medical exam?

+

No medical exam required. That's one of our biggest advantages. No blood tests, no doctor visits, no intrusive health interviews. Just honest answers to straightforward health questions.

We ask about your age, smoking status, and any major health conditions. That's it. Our underwriting process is designed to be simple and respectful, not invasive and complicated like traditional insurance companies.

How does this help my family?

+

Final Expense insurance helps your family in a real, tangible way. When the time comes, your family will have the money they need to:

  • Pay for funeral services and arrangements
  • Cover burial or cremation costs
  • Pay outstanding medical bills
  • Handle other final expenses
  • Avoid financial stress during an emotional time

Your family won't have to worry about where the money is coming from. They can focus on grieving and celebrating your life, knowing you've taken care of them.

How does the claims process work?

+

When someone you've named as a beneficiary needs to file a claim, here's what they do:

  • Contact our partner carrier: They'll reach out to the insurance company with your policy number
  • Provide documentation: They'll need a death certificate and the original policy documents
  • File the claim: Our partner handles all the paperwork and processing
  • Receive payment: Once approved, the death benefit is paid directly to your beneficiary

Our partner carriers are committed to processing claims quickly and respectfully. Most death claims are processed within 2-4 weeks of receiving all required documentation.

What ages qualify for Final Expense insurance?

+

Final Expense insurance is available to adults age 40 through 85. This age range covers most adults looking for affordable end-of-life coverage.

If you're under 40 or over 85, you may still have options. Contact us to discuss your specific situation. We're here to help find solutions that work for you and your family.

What coverage amounts are available?

+

Final Expense insurance coverage typically ranges from $5,000 to $25,000. You choose the amount that makes sense for your situation.

Here's how to think about it:

  • $5,000-$10,000: Basic coverage for funeral services
  • $10,000-$15,000: Covers funeral plus some additional expenses
  • $15,000-$25,000: Comprehensive coverage including medical bills and other costs

Our quote form will help you determine the right amount based on your family's needs and budget.
