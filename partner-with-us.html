For Insurance Carriers

The Opportunity: Self-Made Legends is a digital distribution channel that connects you with qualified Final Expense prospects. We handle the customer acquisition, qualification, and initial intake. You handle underwriting, approval, and claims management.

What We Provide:

  • Pre-qualified customer leads
  • Complete, structured application data
  • Scalable volume with predictable quality
  • Digital integration and reporting
  • Dedicated partnership support

How It Works:

  • Customers complete our online quote form
  • We provide their data to you for underwriting
  • You make approval decisions
  • Customer purchases policy directly from you
  • We handle all ongoing support

For Funeral Homes

The Opportunity: Help families prepare for the inevitable while creating a new revenue stream. When families come to you, you can now offer them a trusted way to protect themselves financially.

What We Provide:

  • Custom QR code for your location
  • Marketing materials for your team
  • Training for your staff
  • Commission on every referral (15% standard)
  • Monthly support and reporting

How It Works:

  • Families come to your funeral home
  • You share our QR code or referral link
  • They complete the quote at home on their phone
  • If they purchase, you earn commission
  • You stay focused on your core service
