About Us

Self-Made Legends is a Black-owned digital insurance brand built to help families create stability and peace of mind. Founded with a vision to empower communities and protect what matters most, we've dedicated ourselves to making life insurance simple, accessible, and affordable for everyone.

Our story is deeply personal. This company is inspired by my daughter and built with the mission of helping families like ours avoid the financial and emotional burden of unexpected costs. We believe that protecting your family shouldn't require complicated paperwork or expensive medical evaluations.

In a world where insurance often feels overwhelming and inaccessible, we're changing the game with modern, AI-assisted solutions that put families first. We're not just selling insurance—we're building generational wealth and legacy for Black families and communities everywhere.

Our goal is to protect families and build generational legacy through modern, AI-assisted insurance solutions.
