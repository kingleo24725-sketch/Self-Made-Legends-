## EMAIL TEMPLATE: CUSTOMIZATION GUIDE

**To personalize any of these emails:**

1. **[Name]** - Replace with recipient's actual name
2. **[Title]** - Their job title
3. **[Company]** - Their organization name
4. **[Carrier/Funeral Home]** - Specify which type of partner
5. **[DATE]** - Meeting or deadline date
6. **[TIME]** - Meeting time
7. **[PHONE]** - Your phone number
8. **[EMAIL]** - Your email address
9. **[WEBSITE]** - Your website URL
10. **[LINK]** - QR code link or landing page
11. **[DOCUMENT]** - Specific deliverable
12. **[CONTACT]** - Support contact information

**Personalization Best Practices:**
- Reference something specific about their organization
- Mention shared values or community focus
- Use their name naturally (not robotic)
- Keep tone professional but warm
- Show you've done research on them
- Respect their time and decision-making process

Example: "I noticed [Funeral Home] has been serving [Community] for [X] years with a commitment to families. That's exactly who we're building Self-Made Legends for..."

## SUBJECT LINE STRATEGIES

**High Open Rate Subject Lines:**
- "Partnership Opportunity - [Their Company]"
- "New Way to Help Families in Your Community"
- "Quick Partnership Question"
- "Following Up - [Company Name]"
- "Let's Grow Together"

**Lower Open Rates (Avoid):**
- "Check This Out"
- "Important"
- "Urgent"
- "Final Expense Insurance"
- "Sales Pitch"

**Best Practice:**
- Keep under 50 characters
- Be specific about the benefit
- Create curiosity without clickbait
- Reference their company name when appropriate

---

## ENGAGEMENT TRACKING

Use these metrics to improve your email campaigns:

**Track These:**
- Open Rate - % who open your email
- Click Rate - % who click your links
- Response Rate - % who reply
- Meeting Rate - % who agree to talk
- Partnership Rate - % who sign agreement

**Target Benchmarks:**
- Open Rate: 30-40%
- Click Rate: 5-10%
- Response Rate: 10-20%
- Meeting Rate: 5-15%
- Partnership Rate: 20-30% of meetings

**If Numbers Are Low:**
- Test different subject lines
- Improve personalization
- Clarify your value proposition
- Check that email isn't going to spam
- Follow up more consistently

---

**Remember:** The goal is genuine connection and mutual benefit, not just volume. Quality personalized emails always outperform mass blasts.
