- Coverage: $25K-$500K

## 5. Phase 4: Agent Network (Months 18-24)

### 5.1 Phase Goals

- Build licensed agent network
- Expand distribution beyond digital
- Create career path at SML
- Increase market penetration

### 5.2 Agent Program

**Agent Types:**
1. **SML Direct Agents** (Employed)
   - Full-time employees
   - Licensed (Life & Health)
   - SML branded
   - Benefits and salary

2. **SML Partner Agents** (Independent)
   - Licensed brokers/agents
   - SML approved
   - White-label partnership
   - Commission-based

3. **Funeral Home Agents** (Embedded)
   - At funeral homes
   - Licensed
   - Funeral home staff
   - SML trained

### 5.3 Recruitment & Training

**Direct Agents:**
- Recruit licensed agents
- Hire from other carriers
- Offer competitive comp
- SML training program

**Partner Agents:**
- Identify independent brokers
- Pitch partnership program
- Provide marketing support
- Offer technology platform

**Funeral Home Agents:**
- Identify motivated staff
- Offer licensing support
- Provide training
- Commission-based model

### 5.4 Agent Support System

- Agent portal (leads, tracking, payouts)
- Marketing materials (co-op program)
- Training & certification
- Ongoing support
- Performance dashboard
