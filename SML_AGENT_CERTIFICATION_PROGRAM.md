## 1. Program Overview

### 1.1 Purpose

The SML Agent Certification Program ensures that every customer-facing agent is trained, competent, and authorized to represent Self-Made Legends with the highest standards of professionalism, product knowledge, and brand alignment.

### 1.2 Program Philosophy

**"Trained, Certified, Authorized to Represent SML"**

All SML agents must:
- ✅ Understand the SML brand, mission, and values
- ✅ Know Final Expense insurance inside and out
- ✅ Master the AI ecosystem and tools
- ✅ Follow all compliance guardrails
- ✅ Interact with customers with empathy and professionalism
- ✅ Use backend systems effectively
- ✅ Know when and how to escalate
- ✅ Collaborate with development teams

### 1.3 Certification Levels

**Level 1: Basic Agent** (New hire certification)
- Completed all 8 modules
- Passed knowledge assessment (80%+)
- Passed practical assessment
- Supervised for first 30 days
- Ready for independent customer interaction

**Level 2: Senior Agent** (After 6 months + 200+ customer interactions)
- All Level 1 requirements
- Additional 6-hour advanced training
- Advanced assessment (85%+)
- Authorized to mentor new agents
- Can make certain decisions independently

**Level 3: Team Lead** (After 1 year + 500+ customer interactions)
- All Level 2 requirements
- Management training (8 hours)
- Leadership assessment (90%+)
- Authorized to supervise team
- Involved in process improvement

## 4. Module 2: Final Expense Product Knowledge

### 4.1 Module Overview
**Duration:** 3 hours  
**Format:** Interactive training, demonstrations, practice scenarios  
**Goal:** Agents can explain Final Expense insurance confidently

### 4.2 Core Content

**Section 1: What Is Final Expense Insurance?**
- Type of life insurance
- Designed to cover end-of-life costs
- Funeral expenses, medical bills, debts
- Peace of mind for families
- Quick payout process

**Section 2: Coverage Details**
- Coverage amounts: $5K, $10K, $15K, $20K, $25K
- Eligibility: Ages 40-85
- No medical exam required
- Health conditions don't automatically disqualify
- Quick approval (usually 3-5 days)

**Section 3: How SML's Process Works**
1. Customer answers 6 questions (2 minutes)
2. SML analyzes info through AI
3. Agent reviews recommendation
4. Customer contacted with next steps
5. Application submitted to carrier
6. Carrier underwrites and approves
7. Policy issued and customer covered
8. Claim process explained and supported

**Section 4: Who Needs Final Expense?**
- Anyone wanting peace of mind
- People with family dependencies
- Those worried about funeral costs
- People wanting to leave legacy
- Those planning ahead responsibly

**Section 5: Common Objections & Responses**

**Objection:** "I don't want to think about death"
```
Response: "I totally understand. It's not fun to think about. 
But here's the gift you're giving your family: when the hard 
time comes, they won't be struggling with finances. They'll 
be able to grieve in peace. That's what makes this so valuable."
```

**Objection:** "Won't it be expensive?"
```
Response: "It's actually more affordable than most people think. 
Final Expense is designed to be economical. Let me show you a 
quote based on your situation. You might be surprised."
```

**Objection:** "I have a health condition"
```
Response: "That's exactly why Final Expense exists. Our carriers 
understand health conditions. Let's see what options are available 
for you. The worst that happens is we learn your options."
```

**Objection:** "What if I get denied?"
```
Response: "Most people don't get denied. But if for some reason 
a carrier decides it's not a fit, they'll explain why. And we 
work with multiple carriers, so we have other options."
```

**Section 6: Carrier Partners**
- SML works with multiple licensed carriers
- Carriers issue policies, not SML
- Each carrier has underwriting appetite
- SML matches customer to best carrier
- Carriers have excellent customer service

**Section 7: Key Distinctions**
- **SML does:** Collects info, analyzes, submits to carriers
- **Carriers do:** Underwrite, approve, issue policies
- **Agents do:** Explain process, answer questions, guide customers
- **Bots do:** Collect structured data, analyze, recommend

**Section 8: Sample Quote Conversation**
```
Agent: "Let me walk you through how Final Expense works at SML. 
It's really designed for peace of mind."

Customer: "How much will it cost?"

Agent: "Great question. Let's figure that out. It depends on your 
age, health, and how much coverage you want. Typical costs are 
$15-30 a month for $10,000 in coverage, but let me see your specific 
situation. Want to answer a few quick questions?"

Customer: "Will I be approved?"

Agent: "Most people are. I can't guarantee approval—the carrier makes 
that decision. But based on what you tell me, I can give you a good idea 
of how likely approval is. Ready?"
```

### 5.2 Core Content

- Analyzes bot outputs
- Never makes final decisions

**Section 2: Agent Bot**
- **Role:** Customer intake and qualification
- **Questions:** 6 required questions
- **Output:** Structured JSON with customer data
- **Integration:** Data flows to backend
- **Customer Experience:** Warm, clear, reassuring
- **Graduation:** Leads to Underwriting Bot analysis

**How to Use Agent Bot Results:**
- Review data quality
- Check for completeness
- Verify customer provided accurate info
- Use as foundation for next steps

**Section 3: Underwriting Bot**
- **Role:** Risk assessment and scoring
- **Input:** Customer application data
- **Output:** Risk score (0-100), recommendation
- **Categories:** Low (0-30), Moderate (31-60), Elevated (61-80), High (81-100)
- **Uses:** Guide agent decision on customer contact

**How to Interpret Underwriting Bot Output:**
| Score | Category | Action |
|-------|----------|--------|
| 0-30 | Low | Typically approve, move to carrier |
| 31-60 | Moderate | Approve with standard conditions |
| 61-80 | Elevated | Request more info or manager review |
| 81-100 | High | Escalate to manager or detailed review |

**Section 4: Claims Bot**
- **Role:** Claims intake and documentation collection
- **Customers:** Grieving families filing claims
- **Output:** Complete claims package ready for processing
- **Tone:** Compassionate, calm, supportive
- **Integration:** Routes to claims specialist

**How to Use Claims Bot:**
- Route customer to Claims Bot
- Monitor for escalations
- Follow up with family
- Track claim status

**Section 5: Fraud Bot**
- **Role:** Anomaly detection and pattern flagging
- **Input:** All applications and claims
- **Output:** Risk flags for investigation
- **Tone:** Factual, observant, non-accusatory
- **Integration:** Routes to fraud analyst

**How to Interpret Fraud Flags:**
- Low risk: Proceed normally
- Medium risk: Verify information
- High risk: Escalate immediately
- Never assume guilt

**Section 6: Compliance Bot**
- **Role:** Monitor all communications for compliance
- **Input:** All customer-facing content
- **Output:** Compliance reports and violation alerts
- **Tone:** Strict, rule-driven, helpful
- **Integration:** Alerts on prohibited language

**How to Work with Compliance Bot:**
- Know what's prohibited
- Include required language
- Don't attempt workarounds
- Ask if unsure

**Section 7: Customer Service Bot**
- **Role:** Answer FAQs and provide support
- **Customers:** Anyone with questions
- **Output:** Helpful, clear answers
- **Tone:** Friendly, patient, helpful
- **Integration:** Escalates complex questions to humans

**How to Use Customer Service Bot:**
- Route simple questions to bot
- Review escalations
- Provide additional support as needed

## 6. Module 4: Compliance & Guardrails

### 6.1 Module Overview
**Duration:** 2 hours  
**Format:** Video presentation, interactive case studies  
**Goal:** Agents understand and follow all compliance requirements

### 6.2 Core Content

**Section 1: Core Compliance Principles**
1. **Transparency:** Be honest about SML and carriers' roles
2. **Honesty:** Never promise what you can't deliver
3. **Respect:** Treat every customer with dignity
4. **Legality:** Follow all insurance regulations
5. **Ethics:** Do the right thing

**Section 2: The Golden Compliance Rules**

**Rule #1: SML Provides Quotes, Carriers Issue Policies**
- Every customer must understand this
- SML does not issue policies
- Licensed carriers underwrite and issue
- This must be stated in first customer conversation

**Rule #2: Never Promise Approval**
- Don't say "You're approved"
- Don't say "Everyone gets approved"
- Don't say "You'll definitely be approved"
- Do say "Most people get approved, but the carrier makes the final decision"

**Rule #3: Never Promise Pricing**
- Don't say "This is the rate"
- Don't say "Guaranteed price"
- Don't say "Locked-in rate"
- Do say "This is an estimate. Final pricing determined by carrier"

**Rule #4: No Medical Advice**
- Don't diagnose health conditions
- Don't recommend treatments
- Don't assess severity
- Do ask facts: "Tell me about your condition"

**Rule #5: No Legal Advice**
- Don't interpret laws
- Don't explain tax consequences
- Don't reference legal requirements
- Do say "That's a question for your attorney"

**Rule #6: Protect Customer Data**
- Never share outside of need-to-know
- Never store on personal devices
- Use strong passwords
- Encrypt sensitive communication

**Rule #7: Include Required Disclaimers**
- "SML provides quotes; carriers issue policies"
- "I can't guarantee approval"
- "All information must be accurate and complete"
- "This is a quote, not a final approval"

**Section 3: Prohibited Language (Memorize)**
❌ "We guarantee approval"  
❌ "You're definitely approved"  
❌ "Everyone gets approved"  
❌ "Best rates guaranteed"  
❌ "This is definitely covered"  

**Section 4: Required Language (Master)**
✅ "SML provides quotes; carriers issue policies"  
✅ "Your approval and pricing determined by the carrier"  
✅ "I can't guarantee approval, but you seem like a good fit"  
✅ "All information must be accurate and complete"  

**Section 5: Compliance Monitoring**
- Compliance Bot monitors all communications
- Daily review of customer interactions
- Weekly compliance reports
- Violations escalated immediately
- Repeated violations result in retraining

**Section 6: What to Do If You Make a Mistake**
1. Stop
2. Admit the error
3. Correct immediately
4. Escalate to manager
5. Document what happened
6. Don't repeat

**Section 7: Case Studies**

**Case Study 1: Approval Guarantee**
```
Agent: "You're definitely going to be approved for this."
Customer: Relieved, moves forward
Reality: Customer is denied, frustrated, feels misled
Violation: Prohibited language, broken promise
Correct Response: "Most people get approved, but the carrier 
makes the final decision based on their review."
```

**Case Study 2: Medical Advice**
```
Agent: "Diabetes is serious, so you definitely need this insurance."
Customer: Feels pressured
Reality: Violates medical practice laws
Violation: Practicing medicine without license
Correct Response: "Many people with diabetes find Final Expense 
helpful for peace of mind. Would you like to get a quote?"
```

**Case Study 3: Data Protection**
```
Agent: Texts customer data to personal phone
Later: Phone is lost
Reality: Customer data exposed, privacy violation
Violation: Negligent data handling
Correct Response: Only use SML secure systems for customer data.
```

### 6.3 Assessment

**Knowledge Quiz:** 20 questions
- Prohibited vs. required language
- Compliance principles
- Data protection rules
- Medical/legal boundaries
- Must score 80%+ to pass

**Case Study Analysis:** Score compliance violations in scenarios

## 7. Module 5: Customer Interaction Skills

### 7.1 Module Overview
**Duration:** 2 hours  
**Format:** Video training, role-play practice  
**Goal:** Agents interact with customers with empathy, clarity, and professionalism

### 7.2 Core Content

**Section 1: The SML Communication Framework**

**Step 1: Acknowledge**
- Recognize their concern or question
- Validate their feelings
- Show you understand

**Step 2: Explain**
- Provide clear, simple answer
- Use plain language
- Break down complex concepts

**Step 3: Reassure**
- Help them feel confident
- Address concerns
- Build trust

**Step 4: Next Step**
- Tell them what happens next
- Set timeline expectations
- Confirm understanding

**Example:**
```
Customer: "Will my health condition prevent me from getting approved?"

Agent: "That's a great question, and I'm glad you asked. [Acknowledge]
Many people worry about this. Here's how it works... [Explain]
The good news is health conditions don't automatically disqualify you. 
We've worked with people in various health situations. [Reassure]
Next, I'll ask you some questions about your health so we can find 
the best carrier fit for you. Ready? [Next Step]"
```

**Section 2: Listening Skills**
- Listen without interrupting
- Ask clarifying questions
- Don't assume
- Take notes
- Confirm understanding

**Bad Listening:**
❌ "I hear you, but here's what you should do..."  
❌ Interrupting customer mid-sentence  
❌ Assuming you know what they mean  
❌ Not taking notes  

**Good Listening:**
✅ Hearing them out completely  
✅ Asking follow-up questions  
✅ Confirming understanding  
✅ Taking detailed notes  

**Section 3: Handling Different Customer Types**

**The Skeptic:**
- Show data and evidence
- Be transparent about process
- Answer all objections
- Move at their pace

**The Emotional Customer:**
- Validate feelings
- Use compassionate tone
- Give them time
- Offer support resources

**The Busy Customer:**
- Be concise and direct
- Respect their time
- Offer call-back times
- Be organized and efficient

**The Knowledgeable Customer:**
- Respect their knowledge
- Don't talk down to them
- Be precise in language
- Engage in dialogue

**Section 4: Managing Emotions**
- Customer frustrated?
  1. Acknowledge frustration
  2. Apologize (for inconvenience, not necessarily your fault)
  3. Explain what happened
  4. Offer solution
  5. Follow through

- Customer grieving (claims situation)?
  1. Express genuine sympathy
  2. Take time with them
  3. Be patient and kind
  4. Offer resources if available
  5. Check in on them

**Section 5: Phone Communication Best Practices**
- Answer within 3 rings
- Smile while speaking (they can hear it)
- Use their name
- Repeat back important info
- Confirm next steps
- Thank them for calling

**Section 6: Email Communication Best Practices**
- Clear, professional tone
- Spell-check before sending
- Include all relevant info
- Set expectations for response
- Professional closing
- Proper contact info

## 9. Module 7: Escalation & Problem-Solving

### 9.1 Module Overview
**Duration:** 1 hour  
**Format:** Case studies, scenario analysis  
**Goal:** Agents know when and how to escalate issues

### 9.2 Core Content

**Section 1: Escalation Decision Tree**

```
Issue Identified
    ↓
Is it within your authority?
    ├─ YES → Solve it
    └─ NO → Escalate
            ↓
    To Manager?
    ├─ Operational issue → Manager
    ├─ Customer complaint → Manager
    ├─ Fraud suspicion → Fraud analyst
    ├─ Legal question → Legal team
    ├─ Compliance question → Compliance team
    └─ Technical issue → Tech support
```

**Section 2: Escalation Types**

**Operational Escalation:**
- High-risk application (score 81-100)
- Data quality concerns
- Missing information
- Unusual circumstances

**Customer Escalation:**
- Customer complaint
- Satisfaction issue
- Special request
- Difficult situation

**Fraud Escalation:**
- Suspicious patterns
- Multiple applications
- False information
- Anomalies

**Legal Escalation:**
- Regulatory question
- Legal interpretation
- Contract issue
- Compliance concern

**Section 3: How to Escalate**
1. Document everything
   - What's the issue?
   - What's been done?
   - Why escalate?
   - What's the urgency?

2. Contact right person
   - Find appropriate contact
   - Explain clearly
   - Provide full context
   - Include deadlines

3. Follow up
   - Check on status
   - Keep customer informed
   - Log outcome
   - Learn for future

**Section 4: Case Studies**

**Case Study 1: Fraud Suspicion**
```
Situation: Application received with same phone number as 
application from last week, but different name and age.

Your Action:
1. Don't process normally
2. Document the matching details
3. Escalate to Fraud Analyst
4. Include both applications
5. Let analyst investigate

Outcome: Analyst confirms duplicate applications, declines both.
```

**Case Study 2: Customer Complaint**
```
Situation: Customer angry that no one called in promised timeframe.

Your Action:
1. Apologize for delay
2. Take responsibility
3. Find out what happened
4. Provide update
5. Escalate to manager if serious
6. Follow up to ensure satisfaction

Outcome: Manager reviews process, adjusts timeline, customer satisfied.
```

### 9.3 Assessment

**Scenario-Based Test:**
- 10 different problem scenarios
- Identify escalation need
- Choose correct escalation path
- Document appropriately
- Score 80%+ to pass

## 12. Continuous Learning Requirements

### 12.1 Annual Refresher Training

All certified agents must complete:
- 2-hour compliance refresher (new regulations)
- 1-hour product update (new features/offerings)
- 1-hour bot system update (system changes)
- **Total:** 4 hours per year

### 12.2 On-Demand Training

Available whenever needed:
- New product launch training
- Bot system changes
- Compliance updates
- New tools or processes

### 12.3 Certification Renewal

**Annual Renewal Process:**
1. Complete refresher training
2. Attestation of compliance
3. Manager performance review
4. Certification renewed (1 year)

**Renewal Requirements:**
- Clean compliance record
- Customer satisfaction scores acceptable
- Performance goals met
- No substantive violations

### 12.4 Decertification

Agents lose certification if:
- Repeated compliance violations
- Customer complaints sustained
- Performance issues unresolved
- Demonstrated lack of knowledge
- Ethical breaches

**Decertification Process:**
1. Manager discusses concerns
2. Improvement plan created
3. 30-day improvement period
4. Reassessment
5. If still failing, decertification
6. Must retrain to recertify
