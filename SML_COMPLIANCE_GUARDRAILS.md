## 1. Compliance Overview

### 1.1 Purpose

The Compliance Guardrails ensure that Self-Made Legends operates legally, ethically, and in alignment with insurance regulations, data privacy laws, and brand values.

### 1.2 Compliance Applies To

✅ **All Bots:** Agent Bot, Underwriting Bot, Claims Bot, Fraud Bot, Compliance Bot, Customer Service Bot  
✅ **All Agents:** Customer service, underwriting, claims, partnership managers  
✅ **All Communications:** Website, email, phone, chat, social media

The **Compliance Bot** continuously monitors all customer-facing communications. All violations are logged and reported to the Compliance Manager weekly.

## 2. Core Compliance Principles

### 2.1 The Five Core Principles

**Principle 1: Transparency**
- Be honest about what SML does and doesn't do
- Clearly state that SML provides quotes, carriers issue policies
- Disclose all material facts
- Don't hide terms or conditions
- Use clear, simple language

**Principle 2: Honesty**
- Never promise what you can't deliver
- Don't misrepresent coverage or benefits
- Disclose limitations
- Admit when you don't know something
- Correct errors immediately

**Principle 3: Respect for Customer**
- Treat customers with dignity
- Don't discriminate based on race, gender, age, etc.
- Respect customer privacy
- Honor customer data
- Solve problems, don't create them

**Principle 4: Legal Compliance**
- Follow all insurance regulations
- Comply with data privacy laws (CCPA, GDPR, etc.)
- Follow anti-discrimination laws
- Maintain proper documentation
- Cooperate with regulators

**Principle 5: Ethical Operations**
- Do the right thing even when hard
- Protect customer interests
- Maintain high standards
- Act with integrity
- Report violations

## 3. SML Company Disclaimers

### 3.1 Core Company Disclaimer

This disclaimer must appear on every quote-related page and communication:

```
⚠️ IMPORTANT DISCLOSURES:

Self-Made Legends (SML) is a technology platform that 
provides free quotes for Final Expense life insurance.

• SML does not issue insurance policies
• Licensed insurance carriers underwrite and issue all policies
• Your SML quote does not guarantee policy approval
• Final pricing is determined by the carrier's underwriting process
• All information you provide must be accurate and complete
• See our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions for full details

SML® is not an insurance company. We connect qualified 
applicants with licensed insurance carriers. Policies are 
issued by our partner carriers, not SML.
```

### 3.2 Page-Specific Disclaimers

**Homepage:**
- "SML provides free quotes. Licensed carriers issue policies."

**Product Page:**
- "Coverage amounts range from $5,000-$25,000. Final pricing determined by carrier."

**Quote Form:**
- "By submitting, you authorize SML to share your information with partner carriers for underwriting."

**Agent Bot Chat:**
- "I'm an AI. I can't guarantee approval. A human agent will follow up with you."

**Email Communications:**
- Each email should include: "SML provides quotes. Carriers issue policies. This is not an approval."

Every form and website must include:

```
Privacy Notice: SML collects your information to provide 
a quote and share with insurance carriers for underwriting. 
We encrypt your data and never sell it. See our Privacy 
Policy for details.
```

## 4. Approval & Pricing Guardrails

### 4.1 What Bots & Agents CANNOT Say

**PROHIBITED:**
❌ "We guarantee approval"  
❌ "Your application is approved"  
❌ "You're definitely approved"  
❌ "We guarantee you'll get covered"  
❌ "Everyone gets approved"  
❌ "You won't be denied"  
❌ "We never turn anyone down"  
❌ "Your coverage is guaranteed"  
❌ "We promise this rate"  
❌ "This is our best rate"  
❌ "You're guaranteed to save money"  
❌ "We lock in the lowest price"  
❌ "This rate is absolutely final"  

### 4.2 What Bots & Agents CAN Say

**REQUIRED LANGUAGE:**
✅ "SML provides quotes. Licensed carriers issue policies."  
✅ "Your approval and pricing will be determined by [Carrier Name]'s underwriting."  
✅ "I can't guarantee approval, but based on your information, you seem like a good fit."  
✅ "This is a quote. Final approval depends on carrier underwriting."  
✅ "Final pricing may differ based on carrier's review."  
✅ "The carrier typically responds within 3-5 business days."  
✅ "All information must be accurate and complete."  
✅ "Your quote is valid for 30 days."  

### 4.3 Underwriting Decision Communication

**For Approved Applications:**
```
"Good news! Based on our review and our partner 
carrier's analysis, we're ready to move forward. 
Here's what your policy would include:

[Coverage details]
[Premium amount]
[Effective date]

This offer is valid for 30 days. Any questions?"
```

**For Pending Applications:**
```
"We're still waiting to hear back from the carrier. 
They typically respond within 5 business days. 
I'll reach out as soon as we hear anything. 
In the meantime, if you have any questions, 
just let me know."
```

**For Denied Applications:**
```
"I have some disappointing news. The carrier has 
decided not to move forward at this time. Here's 
their reason: [Specific reason from carrier]

This doesn't mean you're not insurable. We work 
with multiple carriers, and we can explore other 
options. Would you like to see what else is available?"
```

## 5. Medical & Legal Advice Guardrails

### 5.1 SML Cannot Provide Medical Advice

**PROHIBITED:**
❌ "That health condition means X"  
❌ "You should get treatment for Y"  
❌ "That condition is serious and you need insurance"  
❌ "Your medication might affect approval"  
❌ "You're high-risk because of Z"  
❌ "That disease typically leads to..."  
❌ "You should talk to a doctor about..."  

### 5.2 SML Cannot Provide Legal Advice

**PROHIBITED:**
❌ "This is what the law requires"  
❌ "You're legally obligated to..."  
❌ "The tax consequences of this are..."  
❌ "This is protected under X law"  
❌ "You should consult a lawyer, but my view is..."  
❌ "This is legally binding because..."  

### 5.3 What SML CAN Say About Health

**ALLOWED:**
✅ "You mentioned diabetes. That's something we see fairly often."  
✅ "Can you tell me more about your health condition?"  
✅ "How is your condition being managed?"  
✅ "Do you take any medications for that condition?"  
✅ "When were you diagnosed with that condition?"  
✅ "The carrier will want to know more details about your condition."  

### 5.4 When to Escalate Medical/Legal Questions

If customer asks medical or legal questions:
1. Do NOT attempt to answer
2. Say: "That's a great question for your doctor/lawyer"
3. Direct to appropriate professional
4. Escalate to agent/manager if in customer interaction
5. Log in system

**Example:**
```
Customer: "Will this insurance cover treatments for my 
heart condition?"

Agent: "That's a great question, but I'm not the right 
person to answer that. You should review the policy 
details, and if you have questions, your insurance agent 
can explain coverage specifics. 

What I can tell you is that Final Expense insurance is 
designed to cover funeral and end-of-life expenses. 
For coverage questions, the carrier will have details 
once you're approved."
```

## 7. Data Protection & Privacy

### 7.1 Core Data Protection Rule

**All customer data is sacred. Treat it like your own family's information.**

### 7.2 Data Collection Compliance

**What Data Can We Collect:**
✅ Age (for underwriting)  
✅ Gender (for demographics)  
✅ Smoking status (for risk assessment)  
✅ Health conditions (for underwriting)  
✅ Coverage amount desired (for quoting)  
✅ Name and contact info (for communication)  

**What Data We CANNOT Collect:**
❌ Passwords or PIN codes  
❌ Bank account numbers (unless processing payment)  
❌ Social Security number (not needed for quote)  
❌ Political affiliation or voting record  
❌ Genetic information  
❌ Biometric data (without consent)  
❌ Children's information (unless they're applicant)  

### 7.3 Data Usage Compliance

**We Can Use Data For:**
✅ Provide quote (primary purpose)  
✅ Underwriting (share with carriers)  
✅ Claims processing  
✅ Fraud detection  
✅ Regulatory compliance  
✅ Product improvement  
✅ Analytics (aggregated, anonymized)  

**We CANNOT Use Data For:**
❌ Selling to third parties  
❌ Marketing to third parties  
❌ Sharing with non-partner companies  
❌ Credit reporting (unless customer approves)  
❌ Discriminatory decisions  
❌ Profiling for non-insurance purposes  

### 7.4 Data Retention Compliance

**Customer Records:**
- Retained for 7 years (insurance industry standard)
- Kept secure and encrypted
- Deleted upon written request (CCPA/GDPR)
- Destroyed after retention period

**Bot Activity Logs:**
- Retained for 3 years
- Used for improvement and auditing
- Aggregated for analytics
- Destroyed after retention period

**Claims Files:**
- Retained per insurance regulation (typically 5-7 years)
- Kept confidential
- Accessible to customer upon request

### 7.5 Data Security Compliance

**SML Must:**
- ✅ Encrypt data at rest (AES-256 or equivalent)
- ✅ Encrypt data in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
- ✅ Restrict access (need-to-know basis)
- ✅ Use strong authentication
- ✅ Maintain audit logs
- ✅ Conduct security reviews
- ✅ Have incident response plan
- ✅ Report breaches to customers/regulators

**Agents Must:**
- ✅ Use strong passwords
- ✅ Log out when leaving desk
- ✅ Use VPN on public wifi
- ✅ Don't email data in plain text
- ✅ Don't store on personal devices
- ✅ Don't discuss data in public areas
- ✅ Report security concerns immediately

## 8. Fraud & Compliance Monitoring

### 8.1 Fraud Bot Compliance

The Fraud Bot must:
- ✅ Flag suspicious patterns objectively
- ✅ Document evidence clearly
- ✅ Never accuse without investigation
- ✅ Escalate for human review
- ✅ Maintain confidentiality

**The Fraud Bot CANNOT:**
❌ Deny applications without human review  
❌ Report to authorities unilaterally  
❌ Contact customers about fraud suspicions  
❌ Share fraud information inappropriately  

### 8.2 Fraud Investigation Compliance

**When Fraud is Suspected:**
1. Investigate thoroughly (don't assume guilt)
2. Document all findings
3. Provide due process
4. Escalate if confirmed
5. Maintain confidentiality throughout

**If Fraud is Confirmed:**
1. Deny application appropriately
2. Document reason clearly
3. Escalate to legal if necessary
4. Consider reporting to authorities
5. Update internal systems
6. Learn to prevent future fraud

### 8.3 Compliance Monitoring Program

**Compliance Bot Reviews:**
- All agent communications with customers
- All bot responses
- All website content
- All marketing materials
- All partner communications

**Compliance Reports:**
- Daily: Any violations found
- Weekly: Compliance summary
- Monthly: Trends and patterns
- Quarterly: Deep dive audit

**Action for Violations:**
- Minor: Correction needed, resubmit
- Major: Investigation, retraining, corrected content
- Severe: Escalate to legal, possible reporting

## 9. Claims Handling Compliance

### 9.1 Claims Processing Standards

All claims must be:
- ✅ Processed promptly (no unnecessary delays)
- ✅ Handled compassionately (respect grieving families)
- ✅ Transparent (explain process clearly)
- ✅ Accurate (verify all documentation)
- ✅ Documented (maintain full record)

### 9.2 Claims Denial Compliance

If claim is denied:
- ✅ Provide reason in writing
- ✅ Reference specific policy provision
- ✅ Explain appeal process
- ✅ Keep tone respectful and empathetic
- ✅ Escalate to management
- ✅ Document fully

**Claims CANNOT be Denied Based On:**
❌ Race, gender, age, disability  
❌ Health conditions at time of application  
❌ Wealth or financial status  
❌ Relationship status  
❌ Circumstantial suspicion (must have evidence)  

### 9.3 Claims Communications

**For Approved Claims:**
```
"We've approved your claim. Here's what you can expect:

[Payment amount]
[Payment method and timing]
[Contact for questions]

If you have questions, please call [number]. 
We're here to help."
```

**For Denied Claims:**
```
"We've completed our review of the claim. 
Unfortunately, we're unable to approve it because:

[Specific reason referencing policy]

Here's what you can do:
- Ask for a detailed explanation
- Review the policy provisions
- Request an appeal
- Speak with the claims specialist

[Contact info]"
```

## 12. Compliance Violations & Escalation

### 12.1 Violation Levels

**Level 1: Minor**
- Tone issue
- Formatting error
- Small omission
- Action: Correct and resubmit

**Level 2: Moderate**
- Missing disclaimer
- Slightly misleading language
- Policy procedure not followed
- Action: Investigate, retrain, correct

**Level 3: Severe**
- Prohibited phrase used
- Customer data breach
- Fraudulent activity
- Discriminatory decision
- Action: Immediate escalation, investigation, possible reporting

### 12.2 Violation Reporting

**If You Witness Violation:**
1. Document it (what, when, who)
2. Report to manager immediately
3. Don't repeat or share
4. Follow up on resolution
5. Log in system

**If Compliance Bot Finds Violation:**
1. Halt the communication
2. Notify person responsible
3. Request correction
4. Escalate if severe
5. Document all action

### 12.3 Investigation Process

**For Suspected Violations:**
1. Gather all facts
2. Interview involved parties
3. Review documentation
4. Determine severity
5. Decide on action
6. Document thoroughly
7. Prevent recurrence
8. Follow up on resolution
