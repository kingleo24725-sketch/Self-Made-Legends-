Nail care FAQ

How long do they last?

7 to 14 days with glue, 1 to 3 days with adhesive tabs. Sets are reusable.

Can I return a set?

Custom sets are made to order and can't be returned, but if sizing is off we'll fix it. Sizing kits make it right the first time.

Do you ship everywhere?

Anywhere in the US. Shipping is calculated at invoice.
