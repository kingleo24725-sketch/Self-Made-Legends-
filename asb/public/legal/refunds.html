Deposits, Cancellations & Refunds

Last updated: [date]. Draft for review by Rose Brown and an attorney before launch.

Plain-English summary: your deposit holds your spot and is non-refundable. It comes off your final bill. You can reschedule once for free with 24 hours notice. If we cancel, you get it back.

Booking deposits

To reserve a date and time for any All Shades of Brown (ASB) service, a deposit is required at the time of booking. The deposit amount is shown before you pay. Deposits are non-refundable except as described below, and are always applied toward the final cost of the service.

Rescheduling

You may reschedule one time at no charge with at least 24 hours notice before your appointment. Rescheduling with less than 24 hours notice, or a second reschedule, forfeits the deposit and requires a new deposit to rebook.

Cancellation by you

If you cancel for any reason, the deposit is forfeited. The remaining balance is not charged.

Cancellation by ASB

If ASB must cancel or cannot perform the service (including unsafe weather for outdoor work), you may choose a full refund of your deposit or move it to a new date.

No-shows

If we arrive and cannot access the property or reach you within 20 minutes, the appointment is treated as a late cancellation and the deposit is forfeited.

Final balance

The remaining balance is due when the service is complete, by card on site or by invoice within 3 days. Quotes for organizing, cleaning, handyman, and lawn care are estimates; any change in scope is agreed with you before extra work begins.

Products

Custom press-on nail sets are made to order and cannot be returned. If a set arrives damaged or the sizing is incorrect based on a sizing kit, we will remake it at no cost.

Questions

Contact us at fyi@myasbllc.com before booking if anything is unclear.
