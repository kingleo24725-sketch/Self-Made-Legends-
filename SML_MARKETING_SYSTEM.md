## 1. Marketing Overview

### 1.1 Purpose

The SML Marketing System attracts potential customers, builds brand awareness, and converts prospects into leads that flow into our sales funnel for quote completion and policy issuance.

### 1.2 Core Marketing Philosophy

**"Transparent, Educational, Compassionate"**

- Transparent about what Final Expense insurance is
- Educational about the process and benefits
- Compassionate toward customers' concerns and needs
- No false promises, hype, or pressure tactics

### 1.3 Customer Journey Map

```
AWARENESS STAGE
    ↓
Customer sees SML ad or content
    ↓
CONSIDERATION STAGE
    ↓
Customer learns about Final Expense insurance
Customer evaluates SML vs. alternatives
    ↓
DECISION STAGE
    ↓
Customer visits website or clicks CTA
    ↓
CONVERSION STAGE
    ↓
Customer completes 6-question quote form
Data flows to backend
Lead created in CRM
    ↓
NURTURE STAGE
    ↓
Agent follows up with customer
Answers questions
Guides to next steps
    ↓
ADVOCACY STAGE
    ↓
Customer becomes policyholder
Customer refers others
Leaves positive reviews
```

## 8. Funeral Home Partnership Funnel

### 8.1 Partnership Value Proposition

**For Funeral Homes:**
- ✅ New revenue stream ($50-150 per referral)
- ✅ Customer satisfaction (help at difficult time)
- ✅ Marketing support (we promote SML)
- ✅ Simple integration (QR code)
- ✅ No software required

### 8.2 QR Code System

**How It Works:**
1. SML creates custom QR code for funeral home
2. Funeral home displays code (poster, card, digital)
3. Family scans QR → Opens SML quote form
4. Family completes quote
5. SML tracks funeral home source
6. SML pays commission

**QR Code Design:**
- Black background (#1a1a1a)
- Gold framing
- SML logo
- Simple instruction: "Get your free quote"
- Funeral home name optional

### 8.3 Partnership Recruitment

**Outreach Message:**
```

funeral costs. SML helps them get protected beforehand.

We'd love to partner with you. Here's what we offer:

✓ $[commission] per customer referred
✓ Simple QR code (we handle everything)
✓ Your customers get free quotes
✓ Your business gets revenue
✓ Customers get peace of mind

It's win-win-win.

Interested? Let's chat about the details.

[Agent Name]
SML Team
```
