Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

What we collect

  • Account: email, display name, and a hashed password.
  • Profile you enter: city and region, what you have (car, laptop, and so on), skills, goals, hours, blocked hours, and anything you tell the crew about your day.
  • Activity: plans, which plays you mark done or skipped, earnings you log, notes, chat messages to the crew, challenges, and scores.
  • Receipts: when you upload a payout screenshot, it is sent to our AI provider to read the amount. We store the amount, the source name, a confidence score, and a fingerprint of the image to prevent reuse. We do not keep the image.
  • Push: if you turn on notifications, the subscription token your browser gives us.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos other than receipts you choose to upload, or bank details.

How we use it

To build your daily plan, run the leaderboard, settle challenges, send the notifications you asked for, and improve the crew's suggestions for you over time. Your profile and results are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate plans, debriefs, chat replies, recaps, and to read receipts. Anthropic processes that data under its API terms and does not use it to train models.

What other players see

Your display name, city and region (if you entered them), score, plays done, hours, logged earnings, streak, league, and badges appear on leaderboards. If you win a day, the plays you did and what you logged are shown as the champion's plan. Your email, goals, notes, chat, and crew memory are never shown to others.

Payments

Paid plans are handled by Stripe. We store your Stripe customer ID and plan level, never card numbers.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. Email us to delete your account; everything tied to it is removed within 30 days, except records we must keep for tax or legal reasons.

Security

Passwords are hashed. Sessions expire. Data is stored on servers we control. No system is perfectly secure, so use a password you do not use elsewhere.

Children

Legends Only is for adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will say so in the app.

See also our Terms of Use.
