## 7.1 Brand Foundation

**Name:** Beauty Bond™
**Endorsement:** *from Self-Made Legends*
**Working subtitle:** Dad + Daughter Beauty Bond

**Brand idea:**

> **The bond is the beauty.**

**Positioning:** The beauty app that isn't about looking better — it's about being
closer. Every visual decision serves warmth, safety, and dignity over aspiration.

**Brand pillars:**

| Pillar | Means | Never |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm** | Skin-tone-derived palette, soft light, rounded forms | Cold, clinical, "tech" |
| **Inclusive** | Every tone represented at equal quality | Tokenism, one deep-skin model |
| **Safe** | Visible controls, plain language, calm defaults | Dark patterns, urgency |
| **Real** | Real families, real texture, real skin | Retouched, aspirational, thin |
| **Rooted** | Culture credited, elders honored | Trend-chasing, appropriation |

## 7.2 Logo Concepts

### Concept A — **The Bond Mark** ⭐ *recommended*

Two overlapping rounded forms — a larger and a smaller — meeting in a shared center
that reads simultaneously as a **link**, a **compact case**, and two faces in profile.

```
        ╭───────╮
      ╭─┤       ├─╮
      │ │   ▨   │ │     large form  = parent
      ╰─┤       ├─╯     small form  = child
        ╰───────╯       shared center = the bond
```

- The intersection is filled with the Rose Gold gradient; the outer forms are outline.
- Works at 16 px (intersection alone becomes the app icon glyph).
- **Why it wins:** it's about *relationship*, not cosmetics. It stays true if the app
  grows beyond makeup, and it never dates the way a lipstick icon would.

### Concept B — **The Brush Heart**

A makeup brush whose ferrule and bristles curve into half a heart; the second half is
formed by negative space, completed only by the mark's counterpart.

- Warmer, more literal, more obviously "beauty."
- Risk: reads young/craft-y; harder at small sizes.

### Concept C — **The Shade Arc**

A semicircle of graduated shade dots — depth 1 through 16 — arcing like a sunrise.

- The most explicit inclusivity statement; excellent as a secondary/pattern device.
- Risk: reads as a color-picker UI, weak as a standalone identity.

**Recommendation:** Concept A as primary identity, Concept C as the secondary
"inclusivity device" used in the Cultural Library and marketing.

### Wordmark

`Beauty Bond` set in **Fraunces** Semibold, optical size 48, with the two `B`s
sharing a subtly aligned bowl. Slight negative tracking (−1%). Always sentence-case —
never all-caps, which reads as shouting against the brand's warmth.

### Endorsement lockup (required)

```
   ╭───────╮
 ╭─┤   ▨   ├─╮      Beauty Bond
 ╰─┤       ├─╯      ─────────────────
   ╰───────╯        from Self-Made Legends
```

- Endorsement line: Inter Medium, 42% of the wordmark's cap height, 65% opacity.
- Minimum clear space: 1× the mark's height on all sides.
- The SML endorsement is **required** on: splash, App Store listing, marketing site
  footer, Bond Book back page, press materials, and email footers (`NOTICE.md` §3.3).

### App icon

Rose Gold → Deep Plum gradient field; the Bond Mark intersection centered in Cream.
No text. Ships in light, dark, and tinted variants.

### Logo misuse (do not)

Recolor outside the palette · stretch or skew · add drop shadows or bevels · rotate ·
place on a busy photo without the scrim · outline the wordmark · substitute a
different typeface · combine with Come Up branding or SML Bucks iconography.

icons, **never for body text**. Text on Rose Gold buttons is `cocoa`, not white
(white on Rose Gold fails at 2.4:1).

## 7.4 Typography

| Role | Family | Weights | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Display / headings** | **Fraunces** | 400, 600, 700 | Variable serif — warmth, `SOFT` axis at 30, `WONK` at 0 |
| **Body / UI** | **Inter** | 400, 500, 600 | Excellent legibility, wide language coverage |
| **Child mode** | **Nunito** | 600, 700 | Rounded terminals, friendlier for early readers |
| **Numerals** | Inter, tabular | 500 | Timers, prices, streaks |

Both primaries are open-licensed (SIL OFL) — no per-seat cost, embeddable in the app,
usable in Bond Book print exports.

### Type scale (8 pt grid, 1.25 ratio)

| Token | Size / Line | Weight | Family | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `display` | 48 / 52 | 600 | Fraunces | Welcome, hero |
| `h1` | 32 / 38 | 600 | Fraunces | Screen title |
| `h2` | 24 / 30 | 600 | Fraunces | Section |
| `h3` | 20 / 26 | 600 | Inter | Card title |
| `body-lg` | 18 / 28 | 400 | Inter | Lesson step text |
| `body` | 16 / 24 | 400 | Inter | Default |
| `body-sm` | 14 / 20 | 400 | Inter | Secondary |
| `caption` | 12 / 16 | 500 | Inter | Labels, chips |

**Child mode:** every size +2 pt, line-height +2, family → Nunito, minimum body 18 pt.

**Rules:** Fraunces for emotional/headline only, never body — it tires at length.

RTL mirroring for Arabic and Hebrew.

## 7.5 UI Style

**Surface & form**

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Corner radius | Cards 20 · buttons 16 · chips 999 · sheets 28 (top only) · inputs 14 |
| Elevation | Two levels only. Soft, warm-tinted: `0 2px 8px rgba(61,38,69,.06)` / `0 8px 24px rgba(61,38,69,.10)` |
| Borders | 1 px `rgba(42,31,28,.08)`; **2 px `rose-gold`** for selection |
| Spacing | 8 pt grid: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64 |
| Gutters | 16 px (20 px in child mode) |

**Components**

- **Primary button** — Rose Gold fill, `cocoa` text, 16 radius, 52 h (60 in child
  mode), full-width on mobile. **One primary CTA per screen, always.**
- **Secondary** — transparent, 1.5 px Rose Gold border, Rose Gold text.
- **Ghost** — text only, used for "not now" / dismissive actions.
- **Destructive** — `danger` text on transparent; filled only inside a confirm sheet.
- **Cards** — `warm-white` on `cream`, 20 radius, level-1 elevation, 16 padding.
- **Chips** — 32 h, pill, `caption` type; selected = Rose Gold 12% fill + border.
- **Inputs** — 56 h, `warm-white` fill, 14 radius; focus = 2 px Rose Gold; errors
  appear **below** the field, never as a placeholder replacement.
- **Bond Meter** — Skia ring, 8 px stroke, Rose Gold→gold gradient, animated 600 ms
  `easeOutCubic`.
- **Shade swatch** — 44 px circle, 1 px inner shadow for edge definition on both
  themes; **always paired with its depth number** (color is never the only signal).

**Motion**

| Interaction | Duration | Curve |
|---|---|---|
| Tap feedback | 100 ms | easeOut |
| Screen push | 300 ms | easeInOut |
| Sheet present | 350 ms | spring(0.8, 0.9) |
| Meter fill | 600 ms | easeOutCubic |
| Celebration | 900 ms | spring |
| **Legacy module entry** | **400 ms fade only** | linear |

**Motion rules:** nothing exceeds 900 ms. `prefers-reduced-motion` → all transitions
become 150 ms fades, hero loops become stills. **No celebratory motion in the Legacy
module, ever** (`01` M08) — no confetti near grief.

**Imagery**

- Real families, real homes, unretouched skin — visible texture, pores, freckles,
  scars, eczema, vitiligo, acne.
- **Every collection ships photography of that heritage**; casting is contractual,
  not aspirational.
- Hands in frame — this is a tactile product about people touching each other's faces.
- Lighting: warm, directional, window-like. Never beauty-dish flat.
- **Never:** stock "diverse group laughing at salad," face-tuned skin, aspirational
  wealth signaling, before/after framed as improvement.

**Iconography** — Lucide base, customized: 2 px stroke, rounded caps/joins, 24 px
grid. Beauty-specific icons (brush types, shade drop, bond link) drawn bespoke to
match.

**Dark mode** — `midnight` ground, `plum-soft` surfaces, Rose Gold lifted to `#F0B88E`
for contrast. **Not** a pure-black inversion; the brand stays warm in the dark.

## 7.6 Emotional Tone

**Voice:** A warm, competent older sister who has done this a thousand times and is
genuinely glad you asked.

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Encouraging | Gushing |
| Plain-spoken | Dumbed down |
| Playful | Silly |
| Confident | Bossy |
| Honest | Blunt |

### Voice by mode

| Mode | Register | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Dad + Daughter | Buddy-comedy warmth; dad's inexperience is the joke, never the shame | "Nobody's born knowing what a fluff brush is. Let's fix that." |
| Mom & Me / Legacy | Quiet, reverent, unhurried | "Take your time. She's not going anywhere." |
| Little Legend | Bright, simple, second person | "Wash your hands first! Brushes love clean hands." |
| Solo Glow | Peer-to-peer, capable | "Sheer on the lid, heavier at the lash line." |
| Global Glam | Curious, respectful | "Every place does this differently. Here's how Lagos does it." |

### Copy rules

1. **Praise effort, not appearance.** "You blended that so patiently" > "You look
   gorgeous." This runs all the way into the Compliment Card templates (`01` M07).
2. **Never comment on a body.** No weight, size, shape, or "flattering."
3. **Never manufacture urgency.** No countdowns, no "only 3 left," no streak guilt.
4. **Own our failures.** "Our side, not yours."
5. **Say "we can't" plainly.** "We can't match you confidently in this light" beats a
   confident wrong answer — especially for deep skin, where a wrong match is the
   trust-killer.
6. **Grief gets no exclamation points.**
7. **Explain the why on every permission.** One plain sentence, no legalese.
8. **Second person, active voice, contractions.** "You'll need a clean brush," not
   "A clean brush is required."

### Reference copy

| Moment | Copy |
|---|---|
| Welcome | "Beauty is a language. Learn it together." |
| Empty gallery | "No memories yet. Your first look is one lesson away." |
| Streak broken | "Life gets busy. Five minutes puts you right back." |
| Low-confidence match | "We can't match you confidently in this light. Want to try by a window?" |
| Consent gate | "Your photo is processed and then deleted. We never train on your pictures." |
| Panic pressed | "You're out. We've let your grown-up know. You did the right thing." |
| Child time limit | "Time to put the brushes down. See you tomorrow ✨" |
| Payment failed | "That card didn't go through. Nothing was charged." |
| Legacy entry | "Whenever you're ready." |
| Letter delivered | "She left this for today." |

## 7.10 Brand Guardrails

Never, in any Beauty Bond surface:

- Retouched or face-tuned skin in any asset
- Before/after framed as improvement — it is "with / without makeup"
- Beauty scores, symmetry ratings, glow-up percentages
- Weight, diet, or body-shape language or imagery
- Skin-lightening products or "brightening to lighten" language
- Countdown timers, false scarcity, or streak guilt
- A single deep-skin model used as the whole diversity story
- Cultural motifs used decoratively without the credited source
- Game-economy visuals borrowed from any other SML product
- All-caps wordmark, or the mark on a busy photo without a scrim

## 7.11 Brand in One Paragraph

Beauty Bond looks like a warm room with good window light. Rose gold and deep plum,
cream not white, serif headlines with soft edges over clean readable body text. Real
skin with real texture, real families, hands in frame. Nothing shouts, nothing rushes,
nothing scores you. Buttons are round and generous, motion is gentle and brief, and
the loudest thing on any screen is the one thing you should do next. It feels like
someone who loves you is teaching you something they know by heart.

**Beauty Bond™ — from Self-Made Legends.**
