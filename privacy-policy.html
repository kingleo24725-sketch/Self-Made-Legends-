Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2024

Information We Collect

Self-Made Legends Life & Legacy Insurance Co. collects basic customer information to provide you with insurance quotes and services. The information we collect includes:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Age
  • Gender
  • Smoker status
  • Health conditions
  • Coverage preferences

This information is collected through our online quote form and AI Agent Bot to help us generate personalized insurance quotes for you.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively to provide insurance services, including:

  • Generating personalized Final Expense insurance quotes
  • Processing your insurance application
  • Communicating with you about your quote or policy
  • Improving our services and customer experience
  • Complying with legal and regulatory requirements

We will never use your information for any other purpose without your explicit permission.

Data Security & Protection

All information you provide is stored securely using industry-standard encryption and security protocols. We are committed to protecting your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Our servers are protected by firewalls, access controls, and regular security audits to ensure your data remains safe and secure.

We Never Sell or Share Your Data

Your personal information is never sold to third parties or marketers. Your data is only shared with our insurance carrier partners as necessary to process your insurance application and policy. You maintain complete control over your information.

Any sharing of your information with partners requires your explicit permission and is done only for the purpose of providing you with insurance services.

Analytics & Website Usage

Our website uses basic analytics tools to understand how visitors use our site and to improve user experience. This tracking is non-invasive and does not collect sensitive financial information.

Analytics data helps us understand which pages are helpful, where visitors are struggling, and how we can better serve our customers. All analytics data is aggregated and anonymized.

No Financial Information Collection

We want to be clear: Self-Made Legends Life & Legacy Insurance Co. does not collect credit card numbers, bank account information, or other sensitive financial data through our website.

All payment processing is handled securely by our insurance carrier partners through their own payment systems with full PCI compliance.

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites before providing them with your information.

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • The right to access your data
  • The right to correct inaccurate information
  • The right to request deletion of your data
  • The right to opt-out of certain uses of your data
  • The right to data portability (in some jurisdictions)

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the information provided above.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Self-Made Legends Life & Legacy Insurance Co. may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new privacy policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

Your continued use of our website following the posting of changes constitutes your acceptance of those changes.
