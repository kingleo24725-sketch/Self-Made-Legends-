# Render prompts — how to draw YOUR shoes, not somebody else's

Every batch so far has come back with a real sneaker in it: an Air Force 1, an
Air Griffey, a Cortez, an Air More Uptempo. That is not a prompting mistake.

**Ask an image generator for "a sneaker" and it returns the sneakers it was
trained on.** It has no concept of a shape you own, so it reaches for the
famous one and redresses it. It will keep doing that forever.

The fix is to stop asking for a sneaker and start describing **your** shoe —
in the same neutral construction language a footwear developer uses, which no
brand owns. Paste one of the blocks below, change the colours, and you get a
new colourway of a shape that is already yours.

---

## How to use these

1. Copy a whole block.
2. Change only the **COLOURWAY** line.
3. Never add a brand name, a model name, or "in the style of". That is the one
   instruction that undoes everything else.

If a render comes back with a swoosh, three stripes, giant bubble lettering, a
sawtooth outsole or a visible air window, **discard it**. The generator drifted
back to what it knows. Re-run the same prompt; do not try to edit it out.

## SML 1s — the court low

> Studio product photograph of a low-cut leather court sneaker, side profile,
> plain white background, soft even lighting.
>
> **Construction:** full-grain leather upper with a suede toe overlay,
> five-eyelet eyestay, padded collar, external heel counter, and a cupsole with
> a white wrapped foxing tape running the full perimeter. Flat gum-free rubber
> outsole. Clean panel seams, no decorative overlays beyond those described.
>
> **Branding:** a single diagonal band across the lateral quarter panel. A gold
> script wordmark reading "Sml" on the quarter. Small gold text at the heel
> reading SELF-MADE LEGENDS and EST. MMXXVI. A small gold crown at the heel and
> on the tongue. A small gold star low on the midsole.
>
> **COLOURWAY:** black leather upper, gold band, black outsole.
>
> No logos, no swooshes, no stripes, no lettering on the midsole.

## SML 2s — the cushioned runner

> Studio product photograph of a low-profile cushioned running shoe, side
> profile, plain white background, soft even lighting.
>
> **Construction:** black suede and mesh upper, layered side panels, six-eyelet
> lacing, padded collar and tongue, moulded external heel counter. Thick
> two-part moulded midsole with a small translucent cushioning window at the
> midfoot. Segmented rubber outsole.
>
> **Branding:** a gold script wordmark reading "Sml" across the lateral panel.
> Small gold text at the heel reading SELF-MADE LEGENDS and EST. MMXXVI. A gold
> crown at the heel and on the tongue. A small gold star low on the midsole. A
> gold sweep along the midsole sidewall.
>
> **COLOURWAY:** all black upper, gold detailing, gum outsole.
>
> No logos, no swooshes, no stripes, no large lettering, no oversized panels.

## SML 3s — the heritage runner

> Studio product photograph of a slim 1970s-style retro running shoe, side
> profile, plain white background, soft even lighting.
>
> **Construction:** suede and mesh upper, flat wedge midsole, gum rubber
> outsole with a fine tread. Slim profile, low stack height, simple panel
> construction, no overlays beyond the toe and heel.
>
> **Branding:** raised block letters reading SML across the lateral quarter,
> outlined in gold. Small gold text at the heel reading SELF-MADE LEGENDS and
> EST. MMXXVI. A gold crown at the heel and on the tongue. A thin gold stripe
> along the midsole.
>
> **COLOURWAY:** royal blue suede and mesh, cream block letters, gum outsole.
>
> No logos, no swooshes, no stripes, no sawtooth or herringbone outsole.

## The editions

Same as the SML 2s block, with the graphic added:

**Cherish SML** — *add:* gold and purple feathered wings spreading across the
lateral panel behind the script, and the word "Cherish" in gold script at the
toe.

**SML Rose Golds** — *add:* embroidered red roses with green leaves at the heel
and at the toe, pink laces, pink cushioning window.

**SML Dre Locs** — *not yet drawn on a shape you own.* Use the SML 2s block
with: royal blue and gold colourway, "Dre Locs" in gold script at the toe where
Cherish sits, and "24th St" in small gold text at the heel above the founding
mark. That puts the tribute on a shoe that can actually be made.

## The words that cause the trouble

Never put any of these in a prompt. Each one pulls the generator straight back
to a shoe somebody else owns:

`Air Force` · `AF1` · `Jordan` · `Dunk` · `Air Max` · `Uptempo` · `Griffey` ·
`Cortez` · `Blazer` · `Daybreak` · `Samba` · `Gazelle` · `Stan Smith` ·
`Superstar` · `New Balance` · `550` · `574` · `990` · `Nike` · `adidas` ·
`swoosh` · `three stripes` · `in the style of` · `inspired by`

And these describe features that belong to a specific shoe:

| Avoid | Because it is |
|---|---|
| Giant bubble letters on the side panel | the Air More Uptempo |
| Sawtooth or herringbone midsole edge | the Cortez |
| Visible air window in the midsole | Air Max trade dress |
| A curved sweep from midfoot to heel | a swoosh by another name |
| "AIR" moulded into the sole | Nike's mark on Nike's tooling |

---

## The one that will still catch you out

The generator does not know it has done this. It will produce an Uptempo,
present it as an original design, and there is nothing in the image that says
otherwise. **Check every render against the table above before you get
attached to it** — that is faster than finding out after you have named it.
