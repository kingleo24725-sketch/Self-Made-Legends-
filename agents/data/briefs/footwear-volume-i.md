# Footwear — the design language from the first mockups

Five AI mockups were produced on 27 August 2026. **The renders themselves
cannot be used** — three carry Nike marks or protected silhouettes, and all
five are AI-generated, which means no human authorship and therefore no US
copyright.

**The ideas in them are the founder's and they survive intact.** This file is
what carries forward. Feed it to the design agent as a brief; it produces an
original silhouette wearing this language.

## The names

These are the founder's own and they carry the brand better than any render.

**Queens Edition — "Born Legendary Twins"**
Green patent with gold. Named for his daughters. Twin pair, sold together, a
motif on each. This is the strongest concept in the set and it should be the
one that gets made properly.

**Money Moves Only — "Money Bag"**
Green tumbled leather, gold blocks, gold-flecked translucent sole. Loud, and
knowingly so.

**Self-Made Legends — EST. MMXXVI**
Black / purple / gold, and white / gold. The house pair. **Closest to usable
already** — the mockups for these two carry no third-party marks, and the
silhouette is generic rather than borrowed.

**24th St RIP — "Dre Loc Edition"**
Royal blue and gold, mid-cut with a strap. A memorial piece.

| Sole | Translucent, tinted to the colourway, gold fleck suspended in it |

| Tongue | Edition name, gold on the upper's colour |

**Two colourways carry the house:** black with gold, and white with gold. The
green, the blue and the purple are editions, not the line.

## For the design agent

The silhouette must be **described, never borrowed**. Neutral construction
language a footwear developer uses — and which no brand owns:

> Low-cut cupsole trainer. Six-eyelet eyestay, wrapped foxing tape, moulded
> toe cap, external heel counter, 28mm heel stack falling to 18mm at the
> forefoot. Herringbone outsole with a pivot circle at the forefoot.
> Translucent outsole rubber, tinted, gold fleck suspended.

Nothing in that paragraph belongs to anyone else, and a factory can build from
it.

**Never write** "Air Force 1", "AF1", "Jordan", "Dunk-style", "Air Max",
"Griffey", or "swoosh" — including in a note about what to avoid. The
`no_third_party_marks` eval fails the run on any of them, on purpose.

---

## What has to happen before any of this is real

1. **A footwear developer draws the last and the pattern.** Footwear is not
   apparel; it needs a real last, and no render substitutes for one.
2. **A footwear factory, not a print-on-demand service.** No POD makes shoes.
   Minimums are usually 200–600 pairs per colourway.
3. **Sampling money.** A first sample runs roughly $300–800, and it takes two
   or three rounds.
4. **Class 025 covers footwear** — it is the same class as the clothing
   filing, so the trademark application already in hand reaches it.

Footwear is the most expensive category in the house to enter and the slowest
to get right. **The hoodie should sell first.** These names will still be here.
