How It Works

Getting Final Expense insurance from Self-Made Legends is simple, fast, and stress-free. Here's how the process works.

Our Simple 4-Step Process

1

Learn About Coverage

Start by learning about Final Expense insurance, what it covers, and how it helps your family. Our website and AI assistant explain everything in plain language.

2

Answer Quick Questions

Complete our simple 6-question form or chat with our AI assistant. Tell us your age, health status, and coverage needs. No medical exam required.

3

Receive Your Quote

Get your personalized quote instantly. See your coverage options, pricing, and next steps. No obligation, no pressure to proceed.

4

Complete Your Policy

When you're ready, complete your application through our partner carrier. Fast approval, simple underwriting, and your policy is active.

Designed for Simplicity

Every step of our process is designed to be simple, clear, and stress-free. We understand that thinking about death and funeral costs can be emotional and confusing. That's why we've made everything as straightforward as possible.

  • No Medical Exam: No blood tests, doctor visits, or health interviews. Just honest answers to basic questions.
  • Minimal Paperwork: Our digital process keeps paperwork to a minimum. Everything is done online.
  • Fast Approval: Most applications are reviewed and approved within days.
  • Plain Language: We explain everything clearly, no insurance jargon or confusing terms.

Why Our Process Works
