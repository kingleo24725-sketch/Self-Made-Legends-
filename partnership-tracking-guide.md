## OUTREACH PIPELINE STAGES

Track each prospect through these stages:

### Stage 1: PROSPECT IDENTIFIED
- **Action:** Research company, find decision-maker contact
- **Timeline:** Start
- **Status:** "Researched"
- **Next:** Send initial email

### Stage 2: INITIAL CONTACT SENT
- **Action:** Send personalized initial outreach email
- **Timeline:** Day 1
- **Status:** "Email 1 Sent"
- **Next:** Wait for response or schedule follow-up

### Stage 3: FIRST RESPONSE
- **Action:** They open/click email or reply
- **Timeline:** Days 1-7
- **Status:** "Engaged"
- **Next:** Follow up with meeting request

### Stage 4: MEETING SCHEDULED
- **Action:** They agree to a call or meeting
- **Timeline:** Days 3-14
- **Status:** "Meeting Scheduled"
- **Next:** Prepare for meeting

### Stage 5: MEETING HELD
- **Action:** You have the call/meeting
- **Timeline:** Days 7-21
- **Status:** "Meeting Held"
- **Next:** Send follow-up based on outcome

### Stage 6: FOLLOW-UP SENT
- **Action:** Send meeting recap and next steps
- **Timeline:** Day 1 after meeting
- **Status:** "Awaiting Decision"
- **Next:** Wait for their response

### Stage 7: AGREEMENT STAGE
- **Action:** Send partnership agreement for review
- **Timeline:** Days 7-30 after meeting
- **Status:** "Agreement Sent"
- **Next:** Follow up if no response

### Stage 8: NEGOTIATION
- **Action:** Address questions or requested changes
- **Timeline:** Days 7-14 after agreement sent
- **Status:** "Under Negotiation"
- **Next:** Finalize terms

### Stage 9: SIGNED
- **Action:** They sign and return agreement
- **Timeline:** Varies
- **Status:** "Agreement Signed"
- **Next:** Onboarding

### Stage 10: ACTIVE PARTNERSHIP
- **Action:** Partnership is live and generating revenue
- **Timeline:** Ongoing
- **Status:** "Active Partner"
- **Next:** Monthly monitoring and optimization

### Stage 11: INACTIVE
- **Action:** They didn't respond after final follow-up
- **Timeline:** 30+ days
- **Status:** "Inactive - Check Back Later"
- **Next:** Reach back out in 6 months

## RED FLAGS & INTERVENTIONS

### Red Flag: Low Referral Volume
- **Possible Causes:** Not promoting, staff not trained, unclear value
- **Intervention:** 
  1. Schedule check-in call
  2. Offer additional training
  3. Help with marketing materials
  4. Provide success stories/testimonials

### Red Flag: Low Conversion Rate
- **Possible Causes:** Poor lead quality, process issues, pricing concerns
- **Intervention:**
  1. Review submitted leads
  2. Analyze quote form completion rates
  3. Discuss pricing/commission concerns
  4. Optimize referral process

### Red Flag: No Communication
- **Possible Causes:** Busy, forgotten, facing internal issues
- **Intervention:**
  1. Reach out with check-in email
  2. Offer support or resources
  3. Ask if they need help
  4. Discuss partnership value

### Red Flag: Negative Feedback
- **Possible Causes:** Product issues, support problems, unrealistic expectations
- **Intervention:**
  1. Listen and understand concerns
  2. Address any product/service issues
  3. Provide additional support
  4. Document for improvement

2. **Recognition** - Acknowledge top performers
3. **Support** - Be responsive to their needs
4. **Transparency** - Share performance metrics openly
5. **Long-term Vision** - Discuss future growth together

## COMMISSION TRACKING

### Commission Calculation:
```
Commission Per Lead = Referred Lead Amount × Commission Rate
Example: $100 lead × 15% commission = $15 per lead

Monthly Commission = # Leads Referred × Commission Per Lead
Example: 10 leads × $15 = $150/month
```

### Commission Payment:
- **Frequency:** [Monthly/Quarterly/Annually]
- **Payment Method:** [Check/ACH/Wire Transfer]
- **Reporting:** Provide partner with detailed commission report
- **Documentation:** Keep records for accounting

### Commission Tiers (Optional):
- **Tier 1:** 0-10 leads/month = 10% commission
- **Tier 2:** 11-25 leads/month = 12% commission
- **Tier 3:** 26+ leads/month = 15% commission

## QUARTERLY BUSINESS REVIEW (QBR) AGENDA

Host quarterly calls with top partners:

**Agenda (60 minutes):**
1. **Welcome & Rapport** (5 min)
   - How's business?
   - Any staffing changes?

2. **Performance Review** (15 min)
   - Leads referred this quarter
   - Conversion rates
   - Commission earned
   - Trends and patterns

3. **Success Stories** (10 min)
   - Share testimonials from their referrals
   - Celebrate wins together
   - Recognize top performers

4. **Challenges & Opportunities** (15 min)
   - What challenges are they facing?
   - How can we help?
   - What opportunities do they see?

5. **Growth Planning** (10 min)
   - Next quarter goals
   - New initiatives
   - Marketing plans
   - Support needed

6. **Next Steps** (5 min)
   - Confirm action items
   - Schedule next QBR
   - Final thoughts
