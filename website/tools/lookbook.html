What we are asking

Four questions

Short answers are fine. We are choosing a production partner and would rather hear what you actually run than have you quote what we asked for.

  1. Which lasts and sole units do you already hold that these could be built on? We are not asking you to tool anything for a first season, and we would rather use your library than commission our own. Photos or a sample would help.
  2. What is your minimum order per colourway at this specification?
  3. What does a first sample cost, and how long does it take?
  4. Can you run a flex-tested metallic gold leather or PU? Metallic finishes crack at the flex point, and we would rather specify one you already run than name one blind.
