01 — The House

Nobody handed us a crown.

Self-Made Legends was not founded in a studio. It was founded in the gap between where a person starts and where they refuse to stop — the long, unglamorous middle that nobody photographs.

The lion is not decoration. It is the oldest symbol of a thing that answers to no one. The crown is not inherited here; it is built, stitched, and worn by people who made themselves.

Every garment carries the crest. Every crest is a receipt.

Legends are not born into thrones. They build the room the throne sits in.

Origin

Founded in Missouri. Built for the first generation in the family to buy it themselves.

09 — Questions

Frequently Asked

What does “numbered run” actually mean?

Each drop is produced in a fixed quantity, and every piece carries a tag with its number in that run. When the run is finished, that colorway is not produced again. A restock in a different colorway is a new run with new numbers.

How do the sizes fit?

Apparel is cut boxy with a dropped shoulder and fits true to size. If you want a closer fit, size down one.

Footwear: the Legend 01 Low and the Marble Slide run true. The Crown Runner runs slightly long — take a half size down.

When is the next drop?

Dates are announced to the newsletter first, then on social. The Drop Calendar shows what is open now and what is next.

How long does shipping take?

Orders are packed by hand and ship in 1–3 business days. US delivery is 3–7 business days standard, or 1–2 by express. Standard shipping is free on orders over $150.

International runs 7–28 business days depending on the region. Full rates are on the Shipping & Returns page.

Can I return or exchange something?

30 days from delivery, unworn, tags attached, in the original box. Nothing is final sale — limited runs included.

Your first size exchange is free both ways. If we sent the wrong thing or it arrived damaged, we cover everything.

Start a return through the contact form, or read the full policy.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — Canada, the UK, the EU, and most of the rest of the world.

Customs duties and import taxes are charged by your country and are your responsibility. We cannot predict or refund them. Rates and delivery windows are on the Shipping & Returns page.

How do I care for the velour and the foil crest?

Cold wash, inside out, hang dry. Never put an iron directly on the crest — the gold foil will lift. If you must press the garment, press the reverse with a cloth between the iron and the fabric.

How do I know a piece is authentic?

Every piece ships in a gold foil rigid box with a velvet dust bag and a numbered authenticity card matching the run tag sewn into the garment. If those three things are not present, it did not come from us.

Do you sell wholesale?

Yes, to a limited number of stockists. Use the contact form and choose “Wholesale enquiry”, or write to wholesale@selfmadelegendsz.com for the line sheet and terms.
