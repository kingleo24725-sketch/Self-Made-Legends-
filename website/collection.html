The House Colourways

The same six names run through the socks, the sandals, the beanie, the suits and the dress shoes. That is what makes them one collection rather than six.

  • Royal Black Gold
  • Stealth Black
  • Emerald Green Gold
  • Legacy Purple Gold
  • Ivory Gold
  • Bloodline Red Gold

Two of these are drifting. Across eight sheets the purple is called Legacy Purple Gold, Empire Purple, Empire Purple Gold and Legacy Purple Edition — four names. The off-white is Ivory Gold, Ivory White Gold and Ice White Gold — three. Pick one of each before a single label is printed: a customer who owns two pieces will notice, and a factory given two names for one colour will dye two colours.
